A. OVERVIEW

The U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) deployed a 3D Robotics Solo multirotor small unoccupied aircraft system on June 21, 2018, with a Ricoh GR II digital camera to collect true color photos at the USGS National Crude Oil Spill Fate and Natural Attenuation Research Site near Bemidji, Minnesota, USA. Imagery data were collected during a survey flown about 400 feet above ground surface relative to the launch location.

The raw true color image data were compiled and aligned automatically into a larger stitched image (orthomosaic) using Agisoft Metashape Professional Edition software version 1.5.5.

No ground control points were used during the field survey.


B. SPECIFICATIONS

Camera: Ricoh GRII
Focal Length: 18 mm
Megapixels: 3.2
Resolution: 7928 x 3624 pixels
Optical Sensor Type: CMOS


C. DIRECTORY STRUCTURE
The unprocessed true colors images collected during the survey and a processed orthomosaic are included here. A survey may consist of one or more flights. Directory includes:

20180621_Bemidji-TrueColor-JPG: Subdirectory with original unprocessed imagery .jpg data. Non-useful images such as during launch and landing were removed. The default Ricoh GRII file names were pre-pended with the survey date (YYYYMMDD), site name, camera (RI for Ricoh GRII), and reference to the specific survey area.

AltText.csv: Comma separated values (CSV) file with two columns, FilePathAndName and AltText. Information for each .jpg file is provided as a separate row. File is provided to meet Alt Text imagery accessibility requirements.

20180621_Bemidji-TrueColor-orthomosaic: Subdirectory with processed true color orthomosaic in both tif and jpg formats.

GPX: GPX files for individual drone flights during true color imagery surveys.
