## Core Contributions
1. Introduction of the MANCO Exergetic Framework (MANCO-EX), converting molecular biomarker depletion into a Specific Exergy Loss Index (ΔXd, kJ/mol) via the Gouy-Stodola theorem and Eyring rate theory.
2. Multi-tiered biomarker cascade (methylphenanthrenes, triaromatic steroids, asphaltenic polar anchor) maintaining diagnostic capability across all biodegradation levels (PM 1–10).
3. Calibration on 1,585 physicochemical samples (USGS Bemidji) and 311 GC-MS chromatographic runs (USGS PGRL).
4. Validation against N = 41 multi-basin samples (Orinoco, Athabasca, Junggar, Bongor).
5. REML-estimated Linear Mixed-Effects Model achieving R²_conditional > 0.99 (MAE < 0.01 ln cP).
6. Critical exergy threshold (ΔXd_crit = 8.50 kJ/mol) defining the Thermodynamic Inutility Boundary.
